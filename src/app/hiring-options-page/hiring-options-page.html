06 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

Every engagement is shaped around its agreed scope. Here are a few questions to help you prepare for the conversation.

Ask HHM a question →
How do I choose between hourly, part-time and full-time support? +

Consider how often the work occurs, how much capacity is needed, how predictable the workload is and whether you need ongoing dedicated support. HHM can discuss these requirements with you before recommending a suitable arrangement.

Are the prices fixed for every engagement? +

The displayed figures are indicative brochure rates. Final pricing should be confirmed with HHM and may depend on the scope, number of entities, transaction volumes and required support.

Can HHM work alongside our existing team? +

Yes. The Extended Team model is designed for collaboration with an existing team. Responsibilities, workflow and communication arrangements should be agreed before work begins.

Can we outsource an entire accounting or payroll workflow? +

HHM's Outsourcing Partner model can cover agreed accounting, payroll and tax activities. The precise responsibilities, deliverables and boundaries depend on the scope agreed for your engagement.

What if our requirements or workload change? +

Discuss changes with HHM so the scope and level of support can be reviewed. Any change to the agreed arrangement, capacity or pricing should be confirmed between both parties.

How are systems, access and responsibilities handled? +

These requirements should be reviewed during onboarding. The parties can agree on the systems involved, appropriate access, workflow ownership, handover information and review arrangements before the work starts.

Is backup professional support available? +

HHM's brochure describes backup professional support availability for its hourly and dedicated-professional options. The precise arrangement should be confirmed in your proposal.
