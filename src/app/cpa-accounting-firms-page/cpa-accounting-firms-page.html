07 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Support for
accounting firms, explained.

Answers to common questions about working with HHM as an additional accounting support partner.

Discuss another question →
Can HHM work alongside our existing accounting team? +

Yes. Under an Extended Team arrangement, HHM can support agreed activities alongside your existing team. Responsibilities, task ownership and communication arrangements are defined before work begins.

What accounting activities can HHM support? +

Depending on the agreed scope, support may include bookkeeping, reconciliations, month-end closing, financial reporting, payroll, tax preparation support, AP and AR management and defined project work.

Can HHM support additional workloads during busy periods? +

Additional capacity can be discussed for recurring or time-bound workload requirements. Availability, required skills, timelines and responsibilities need to be agreed before assignments are accepted.

Who reviews the accounting work before delivery? +

The proposed delivery workflow can include preparation, reviewer checks and manager sign-off before the agreed output is passed forward. The client's designated representative retains any required final approval or client-facing authorisation.

Can our firm retain control of client communication? +

Communication responsibilities can be agreed during onboarding. Your firm can retain client relationship management while HHM supports the internal work assigned under the engagement.

Which engagement models are available? +

HHM offers Extended Team, Outsourcing Partner and Project-Based Support models. The suitable model depends on the activities, required capacity and intended working relationship.

How do we discuss pricing and staffing requirements? +

Share your expected workload, activities, transaction volumes and required capacity. HHM can then discuss suitable staffing options and pricing based on the agreed scope.
