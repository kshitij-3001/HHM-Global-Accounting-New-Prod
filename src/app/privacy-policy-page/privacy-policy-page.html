01 / OVERVIEW

About this policy

In this policy, “HHM Global Accounting”, “HHM”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the business operating this website. This policy applies to personal information handled through the website and related enquiries. Separate terms or notices may apply to specific client services, employment processes or other engagements.

This page is a general draft. Before publication, HHM should verify it against its actual website technology, contact forms, analytics, recruitment processes, vendors, locations and applicable legal obligations.

02 / INFORMATION

Information we may collect

The information collected depends on how you interact with us. It may include:

  • Contact and enquiry information: your name, business name, email address, telephone number, role and the details you choose to include in a message.
  • Business communication: information provided when discussing a service, requesting a quotation, arranging a meeting or corresponding with our team.
  • Recruitment information: information you submit if you apply for a role or internship, where that process is offered through our website or related channels.
  • Technical information: information that may be recorded by the website host or security tools, such as IP address, browser and device details, request timestamps, pages requested and diagnostic or security logs.
  • Cookie or analytics information: information collected through cookies or similar technologies, if these tools are enabled on the website.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through a general enquiry form unless it is necessary and a suitable method has been agreed.

03 / PURPOSE

How information may be used

Depending on the interaction and applicable law, information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate about requested services.
  • Prepare proposals, coordinate meetings and manage business relationships.
  • Review applications or other information submitted for recruitment purposes.
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot and help secure the website and our systems.
  • Prevent misuse, investigate potential security incidents and protect legitimate business interests.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, recordkeeping or dispute-resolution requirements.

Where applicable law requires a lawful basis, notice or consent for a particular use, HHM should identify and follow the requirement that applies to that activity.

04 / COOKIES

Cookies and analytics

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to support essential website functions, remember preferences, protect the site or understand website usage if those tools are enabled.

The specific cookies, analytics services and retention periods must be confirmed against the website's live configuration. Where consent is required for optional cookies or analytics, the applicable consent controls should be provided before those technologies are used.

05 / DISCLOSURE

How information may be shared

HHM does not need to share personal information for every purpose listed in this notice. Where sharing is necessary and permitted, recipients may include:

  • Website hosting, IT, security, communication or other service providers who help operate the website or support business operations.
  • Professional advisers, auditors or insurers where appropriate.
  • Relevant parties where needed to respond to a legal obligation, lawful request, dispute or security incident.
  • A successor or relevant transaction party if the business undergoes a reorganisation or transfer, subject to applicable safeguards.

Service providers should be reviewed to confirm their role, access, contractual obligations and whether they process information on HHM's behalf.

06 / SAFEGUARDS

Retention and security

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including applicable legal, accounting, recordkeeping and dispute-resolution requirements. Actual retention periods depend on the information type and business process.

We aim to use reasonable technical, organisational and access controls to protect personal information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and any security practices described here should reflect the controls actually maintained by HHM and its providers.

07 / LOCATION

International processing

Depending on the systems and service providers used, personal information may be processed or accessed in a country other than the country where you are located. Where international transfers are subject to legal requirements, the appropriate transfer mechanism or safeguards should be used.

Before publication, HHM should confirm the countries in which website, email, analytics, recruitment and business systems store or access information.

08 / YOUR CHOICES

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and the law that applies, you may have rights to request access to personal information, seek correction or deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a complaint with a relevant privacy regulator.

To make a request, contact HHM through the website's contact page and begin your message with “Privacy Request.” We may need to verify your identity and clarify the request before responding. Not every right applies in every circumstance, and legal exceptions may apply.

09 / EXTERNAL LINKS

Third-party websites

This website may contain links to websites or services operated by others. Their privacy practices are controlled by their own operators. Review the applicable privacy notices before providing personal information to a third party.

10 / UPDATES

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our practices, website tools or legal obligations change. The date near the top of the page should be updated when a revised version is published.

For a privacy-related question or request, use the HHM Global Accounting contact page and include “Privacy Request” in your message so it can be directed appropriately.
