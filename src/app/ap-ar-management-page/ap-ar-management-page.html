07 / COMMON QUESTIONS

AP & AR support
explained.

Answers to common questions about supplier payments, customer collections and accounts management support.

Ask us a question →
What is included in Accounts Payable support? +

Depending on the agreed scope, support can include supplier invoice processing, verification, coding, approval coordination, payment schedule preparation and vendor statement reconciliations.

What is included in Accounts Receivable support? +

Support can include customer invoicing, invoice tracking, receipt allocation, customer account reconciliations, aging reports and approved collection follow-up coordination.

Can HHM manage both AP and AR activities? +

Yes. Both workflows can be discussed together or separately. Activities, responsibilities and reporting expectations are agreed before an engagement starts.

Does HHM directly approve or release supplier payments? +

Invoice preparation, verification and payment schedule support can be included in the agreed workflow. Final payment authorisation and release remain with the client's designated authorised personnel unless a different arrangement is expressly agreed and appropriately controlled.

Can HHM help with overdue customer balances? +

HHM can support the preparation of aging reports, outstanding balance lists, customer account reviews and follow-up coordination based on the client's approved communication process.

How do we get started? +

Contact HHM with details about your supplier and customer workflows, transaction volumes, accounting systems and support requirements. We can then discuss the scope and possible working arrangement.
