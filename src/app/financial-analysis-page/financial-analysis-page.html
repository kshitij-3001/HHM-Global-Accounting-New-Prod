06 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Financial analysis
explained.

A few common questions about the reporting and planning activities you can discuss with HHM.

Ask HHM a question →
What is included in financial analysis support? +

The scope can include financial reporting, performance comparisons, variance analysis, budgeting, forecasting, cash-flow management and financial modelling. Specific deliverables are agreed before work begins.

Can HHM prepare management reports? +

HHM can discuss the preparation and presentation of agreed financial reporting and management information. The required formats, reporting frequency and information to be included should be defined in the engagement scope.

Can you help with budgeting and forecasting? +

Yes. Budgeting and forecasting support can be discussed using available financial information, business inputs and agreed assumptions.

Can cash-flow information be included in reporting? +

Cash-flow information, expected inflows and outflows, and forecasting activities can be included when they are part of the agreed scope and the necessary information is available.

How do we get started? +

Contact HHM and explain the reporting or analysis requirement. The team can discuss the available information, expected deliverables and suitable engagement arrangement.
